“WHERE DO I START WITH AI?”

One useful task.
A confident first step.

You don’t need to overhaul the business. Start with a problem your team knows, prepare the right information and learn from a small pilot.

01 / CHOOSE THE WORK

Where should we start?

Pick a frequent, repetitive task with a clear result: answering routine questions, preparing an invoice or gathering a weekly update. Agree who owns it and what improvement you want to see.

02 / PREPARE THE BUSINESS

How do we get AI-ready?

Bring together your current instructions, approved answers and useful examples. Map the tools involved and decide what information AI may access. Clear information comes before more software.

03 / BRING PEOPLE ALONG

How do we introduce it?

Choose the pilot with the people doing the work. Practice together, show how to check the output and keep a person responsible for exceptions. Expand when the team can see it helping.
